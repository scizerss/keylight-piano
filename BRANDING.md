# Name and branding

Keylight's code is free software under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may use, copy, change and
share it, including commercially, as long as you keep the copyright notice and the licence text.

This note is about something the licence does not cover: the **name and identity** of the project.

## What belongs to the project

- The name **Keylight**, and names that could be mistaken for it (for example "Keylight Piano",
  "Keylight Pro" or "Keylight 2").
- The Keylight icon and logo (`assets/icon.png` and the artwork made from it).
- The official website, https://keylightpiano.app, and the project's accounts: the GitHub
  repository `scizerss/keylight-piano` and the Ko-fi page.

## What this means for forks and copies

If you publish your own version of the code, on an app store or anywhere else:

- Give it **a different name and a different icon**.
- Do not say or suggest that it is the official Keylight, or that it is made, approved or
  supported by the Keylight project.
- Keep the copyright notice in `LICENSE` and the notices in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
  Those are required by the MIT licence and by the licences of the songs and sounds it includes.
- Do not use the project's screenshots or videos to advertise your version as the original.

You are welcome to say that your version is **based on Keylight** and to link to this repository.

## Official downloads

The official apps are published only from:

- the [releases page](https://github.com/scizerss/keylight-piano/releases/latest) of this repository,
- the website https://keylightpiano.app, which links to those releases, and
- for iPhone and iPad, the App Store listing published by **scizerss** and linked from the website.

A copy of Keylight from anywhere else is not an official build.

## Not affiliated with ROLI

Keylight is an independent community project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by ROLI.
ROLI, LUMI and the names of other products mentioned here belong to their owners and are used
only to say what Keylight works with.

## Questions

Open an issue at https://github.com/scizerss/keylight-piano/issues if you are unsure whether a
use of the name is fine. Reasonable uses, such as a review, a tutorial, a translation project
or a clearly named fork, are almost always fine.
